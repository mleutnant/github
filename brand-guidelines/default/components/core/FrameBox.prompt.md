The brand's signature layout device: a filled field with a frame crossing it, leaving a gap.

```jsx
<FrameBox surface="blue" frame="yellow" corner="bottom-right">
  <h2 style={{color:'var(--text-on-blue)'}}>System QuinLine®</h2>
</FrameBox>
```

Use one per view, at a generous size. On full-bleed photography the same principle may be built from two frames instead of a frame plus a field. Frame colours: yellow or red on blue fields, red or yellow on photos.
