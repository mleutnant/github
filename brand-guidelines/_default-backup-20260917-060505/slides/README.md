# Slide templates

Recreated from the PowerPoint Vorlage documented in the Corporate Design Guideline, p.11.

| File | Slide type |
|---|---|
| `TitleSlide.html` | title: logo top-left, title over a red rule, photo right, yellow rule crossing it |
| `ChapterSlide.html` | chapter divider: blue field, white display title, yellow foot rule, red corner |
| `IconGridSlide.html` | product arguments as icon + caption on blue, product photo overlapping the white edge |
| `ThreeColumnSlide.html` | three bullet columns on blue plus a row of detail photos |
| `FactsSlide.html` | white "Fakten und Zahlen" grid |
| `ImageTextSlide.html` | photo left, headline + level-1 bullets right |
| `ClosingSlide.html` | thank-you slide with contact block |

Anatomy that repeats on every slide: 1280 × 720; logo at 26 px height, 40 px from the top, 56 px from
the edge; slide number and date bottom-left ("8 | 21.03.2024"); a yellow 8 px rule closing the foot of
every blue field; a red L-corner where the blue field meets the white margin. Titles are bold with
115 % leading, body copy Medium at 145–150 %.
