Renders the SCHMIDT logo; use `theme="negative"` on blue surfaces or photos, never a recoloured mark.

```jsx
<Logo height={34} />
<Logo variant="mark" height={28} />
<Logo theme="negative" height={28} />
```

The logo is always positive (red mark + black wordmark) on white by default. Negative = all white. Mono = all black ("Schwarz auf Weiß"). Height is driven by the Bildmarke height; print sizes are fixed (Briefbogen A4 8.681 mm, Visitenkarte 6.628 mm, Magazin 7.246 mm). Keep a square of clear space equal to the Bildmarke height around the lockup.
